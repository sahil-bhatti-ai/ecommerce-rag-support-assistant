# Product FAQ

## Warranty
Most electronics products include a 12-month limited warranty from the date of delivery. Warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, unauthorized repair, or normal wear and tear.

## Missing items
If an accessory or item is missing from the package, customers must report it within 48 hours of delivery. The customer should provide the order ID, unboxing images if available, and photos of the received package contents.

## Wrong item delivered
Wrong item complaints must be reported within 7 days of delivery. Support should verify the SKU ordered, SKU delivered, product photos, and invoice details before arranging replacement or return pickup.
